Why AirCopy

Built for fast, local clipboard handoff.

Works across your Macs

Install AirCopy on every Mac you use. Once they discover each other, copy and paste flows naturally.

No shared Apple account

AirCopy does not depend on Universal Clipboard or iCloud. Nearby peer discovery is enough.

Image support included

Screenshots, copied images, and text clips all stay available in the app and the menu bar.

Quick verification

Check peers, recent items, and restore any of the last three clips without digging through settings.

Privacy

Your clipboard stays between your Macs.

AirCopy is designed around local discovery and direct peer sync. It is meant for nearby devices you control, not a cloud clipboard service.
